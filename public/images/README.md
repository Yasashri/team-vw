# Image folders

The project intentionally does not hotlink Google Sites media. Replace placeholders with approved original, high-resolution lab assets.

Recommended folders:

- `team/` — portraits, ideally square or 4:5, minimum 1000 px on shortest side
- `research/` — research figures / conceptual artwork; keep captions and permissions documented
- `publications/` — graphical abstracts or cover art only when reuse is permitted
- `news/` — awards, meetings and announcement photography
- `lab-life/` — group meals, travel and social photography
- `branding/` — Team VW-owned brand graphics

Prefer AVIF or WebP for photography. Keep an original archive outside the production repo.
